Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy notice — verify before production publication. This notice describes the current static website code. Actual hosting/logging, domain services and any later integrations must be checked and updated by the site operator. No unverified claim of GDPR compliance is made.

1. Controller

Moayad Almohaishi, Bayreuth, Germany. Contact: moayad@almohaishi.dev. The controller's full postal address must be completed before this notice is considered final; see Impressum.

2. Hosting and technical access data

When you access the website, the hosting provider may process your IP address, request date and time, requested URL, browser information and security-related logs to deliver and protect the website. The exact provider, hosting location, legal basis, retention period, subprocessors and any international transfers require verification against the production hosting configuration and provider contract.

3. Website functionality and local storage

The site includes a light/dark theme switch. When you select a theme, the preference is stored locally in your browser under the key theme. This preference is used only to remember your chosen display mode; the website code does not transmit it to its operator. Browser storage can be cleared in your browser settings. The page should be assessed for the applicability of § 25 TDDDG and its exemptions; non-essential tracking or storage would require a separate legal assessment and, where applicable, consent.

4. Curated questions about my work

The “Ask about my work” panel is a locally implemented, fixed-answer FAQ. It does not submit questions to an AI model or website backend. Selecting a question displays predefined information inside the browser. This notice must be updated before a hosted chatbot, conversation logging or AI API is activated.

5. Contact by email

When you contact the operator using your own email application, your email address, message and any other data you include are processed to handle your inquiry. Depending on the communication, the lawful basis may be Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (responding to inquiries). The relevant email provider and retention schedule must be confirmed and documented.

6. Links and external services

The website links to GitHub, Prompt Studio, n8n documentation and other third-party websites. Clicking an external link transfers you to that operator, whose privacy policies apply. The current static website code contains no active newsletter form. A future newsletter requires additional disclosures and a suitable opt-in process before collecting addresses.

7. Legal bases and retention

Depending on the processing purpose, technical delivery/security generally relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, while communications may rely on Article 6(1)(b) or (f). Actual logging and retention periods must be obtained from the host and email provider rather than assumed. Personal data should be retained only as long as necessary or legally required.

8. Your rights

Subject to the legal conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing under GDPR Articles 15–21. You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA) where appropriate. Contact the controller using the email above to exercise your rights.

9. Changes to this notice

Last revised: 12 November 2026. Review this statement when changing hosting, analytics, fonts, embedded media, newsletter or AI features.
